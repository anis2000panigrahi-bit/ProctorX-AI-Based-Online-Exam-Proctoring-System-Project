Examination Guidelines & Instructions

Please read all the rules carefully before starting your examination.

01Use a quiet, well-lit room and ensure your face is clearly visible to the webcam throughout the examination.
02Allow webcam and microphone access when requested by the browser. Camera monitoring is required for AI-based proctoring.
03Ensure that your internet connection is stable before starting the examination.
04Do not leave the examination page or switch to unauthorized applications during the test.
05Do not use mobile phones, books, notes, websites, or other unauthorized materials during the examination.
06Only the registered student is permitted to appear for the examination. Do not allow another person to assist you.
07Keep your face properly positioned in front of the camera. Avoid frequently moving away from the camera.
08Read every question carefully before selecting an answer or writing a descriptive response.
09For short-answer questions, select only one option that you believe is correct.
10For long-answer questions, write clear, relevant, and well-structured answers in your own words.
11Review your answers before final submission. Once submitted, you cannot modify your responses.
12Wait for the submission confirmation and result or submission status after completing your examination.
